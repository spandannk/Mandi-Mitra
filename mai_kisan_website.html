The Problem

Indian Farmers Lose ₹1 Lakh Crore+ to Middlemen Every Year

When a farmer sells tomatoes at ₹12/kg in mandi, the same tomato reaches Delhi at ₹45/kg. The farmer gets just 27% of the final price. This information gap is the biggest injustice in Indian agriculture.

😔

No Price Transparency

Farmers have no easy way to compare mandi prices with urban retail prices in real time.

📉

Forced to Sell Low

Without market intelligence, farmers accept whatever price middlemen offer at the mandi gate.

Long Supply Chain
